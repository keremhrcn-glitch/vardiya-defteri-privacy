Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026
Application ID: com.vardiyadefteri.app

Shift Ledger (Vardiya Defteri) is an offline Android app for keeping track of work shifts and overtime. This policy describes the data practices of app version 0.3.2.

1. Information processed

  • Shift dates, types, start and end times, and days off
  • Overtime entries, notes, and payment statuses
  • Hourly rate, overtime multiplier, currency, and other calculation preferences
  • Repeating shift patterns and reminder preferences

You enter this information to use the calendar, reporting, shift reminders, and estimated pay features.

2. Storage, collection, and sharing

This version does not use user accounts, a developer-operated server, advertising SDKs, or analytics SDKs. App records are stored locally in the app's device storage and are not automatically sent to the developer. The app does not share your information with third-party advertisers.

3. Notifications and device events

Optional shift reminders may request Android notification permission. The app responds to device restart, time changes, and time-zone changes to schedule reminders again.

4. CSV and JSON files

You can export reports and backups, and import data using the Android file picker. You choose where files are stored. If you upload or share files using a cloud or third-party service, that provider's privacy and security practices may apply. Please protect any exported files.

5. Security and retention

Records remain in the app's local storage until you delete them or clear the app's data. The app relies on Android's device-level protections. No electronic storage method guarantees absolute security.

6. Deleting information

You can delete individual shift and overtime records inside the app. To erase all locally stored app records, clear the app's storage in Android Settings. Exported CSV and JSON files must be deleted separately. No account is required.

7. Changes

We will revise this policy if app functionality or data-handling practices change and update the date above.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Shift Ledger contact page (GitHub Issues). GitHub issues may be public, so do not include personal or sensitive information.

A developer support email will be designated before the Google Play release.
